# The floor power of a base-2 repunit

Status: **REPUNIT_FLOOR_POWER_IS_CLOSED_FORM**

Generated by `python -m research.juggler_sequence.mersenne_floor_power`.

## Closed forms

- even `a`: `floor((2^a - 1)^(3/2)) = 2^(3a/2) - 3*2^(a/2 - 1), even a`, bits `1^(a-1) 0 1 0^(a/2 - 1)` -- checked to `a = 400`: `True`
- odd `a`: `floor((2^a - 1)^(3/2)) = floor(sqrt 2 * K), K = 2^((3a-1)/2) - 3*2^((a-3)/2), odd a` -- checked to `a = 199`: `True`
- top of cell 2^(3a/2) minus the image is exactly 3*2^(a/2 - 1)
- the digits are binary digits of sqrt 2 -- the same hand-over the exponent bridge found at the end of an exact even tower, here off the perfect-power locus

## The whole run, in closed form

- `T^j(2^n - 1) = 3^j * 2^(n-j) - 1 for 0 <= j <= n` -- checked to `n = 199`: `True`
- in binary: bits of 3^j - 1 followed by exactly n - j ones
- the trailing-one block is the number of steps remaining, so Lemma 8's countdown is visible in base two at every step of the run, not only at its ends
- `n = 4`: `['1111', '10111', '100011', '110101', '1010000']`

## Squarefree is stronger than we need

- all known M_p with p prime are squarefree; Guy (1994) believes some are not -- so squarefreeness of Mersenne numbers is OPEN
- what we need: not a perfect power, which Catalan (mihailescu-2004-catalan) gives unconditionally for every a >= 2
- squarefreeness already fails at `[6, 12, 18, 20, 21, 24, 30, 36, 40, 42, 48, 54]`
- the squarefree route would fail at a = 6 and is open where it does not; Catalan covers every index, so the weaker property is the right tool

## Bang's theorem, and why a = 6 is the named exception

- `2^n - 1 = prod over d | n of Phi_d(2)`: `True`
- Bang: 2^n - 1 has a primitive prime divisor for every n except n = 1 and n = 6 -- indices with no primitive divisor here: `[1, 6]`, as stated: `True`
- the collision: `Phi_2(2) = 3` and `Phi_6(2) = 3`, so `M_6 = 1 * 3 * 7 * 3 = 3^2 * 7`
- the same collision kills the primitive divisor at n = 6 and creates the square, so the squarefreeness witness a = 6 is Bang's named exception
- Bang and Zsigmondy move ONE exponent; the cycle gap 3^o - 2^K moves two and is not a Cunningham number, which is why primitive-divisor theory does not reach it

## Two coincidences that are not content

- Fermat-polynomial coefficients: NUMEROLOGY. The characteristic polynomial is (t-1)(t-2) = t^2 - 3t + 2 because 2^n - 1 is a combination of 1^n and 2^n, so the 3 is the trace 1 + 2 and the 2 is the determinant 1 * 2. Neither is the 3 of 3x + 1.
- A020914 length: RESTATEMENT. A020914(n) is by definition the binary length of 3^n, so this says only that the endpoint is 3^n - 1. The Mersenne word length n + v_2(3^n - 1) is unrelated to A020914(n): at n = 3 it is 4 against 5, at n = 5 it is 6 against 8.
- Cunningham: the cycle gap 3^o - 2^K is NOT a Cunningham number -- two bases and two independently moving exponents -- which is the precise reason classical primitive-divisor theory (Zsigmondy, Bang, Carmichael) does not reach it. A citable reason rather than a guess.

## Lemma 8 in base two

- `run(x)` is the trailing-one count of `x`, odd `x < 300000`: `True`
- the floor is attained only at the repunits: `[1, 3, 7, 15, 31, 63, 127, 255, 511, 1023, 2047, 4095]`
- M_a = (1^a)_2 maps in a steps to 3^a - 1 = (2^a)_3 -- `True`
- exactly v_2(3^a - 1) halvings follow: 1 for odd a, 2 + v_2(a) for even a -- `True`

## The primality is decorative

- attained at composite `a` too: `True`
- `M_a` is never a perfect power for `a >= 2`: `True` (Catalan, proved by Mihailescu: 2^a - c^k = 1 has no solution with c,k >= 2)
- exactRun(M_a) = 0 for a >= 2: the repunits are maximally inexact starts
- the extremal object is u = x+1 = 2^a; under the exponential bridge it transports to the exponent e = 2^r, one one-bit, not a repunit, so the all-ones pattern is coordinate-dependent and carries no prime content

## What this does not say

One family of density zero. No bound moves, no cycle is touched, N_0 is unchanged, and nothing here is a halt theorem. The base-2 reading of Lemma 8 and the repunit-to-repdigit landing are elementary restatements of the congruence and are recorded as such; the closed forms are new to this repository, where every other exact floor-power value sits on the perfect-power locus. The Mersenne name carries primality, the mathematics does not: the bound is attained at 2^a - 1 for every a.
