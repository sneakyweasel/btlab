# Research modules

Active applications:

- `research.juggler_sequence`: Juggler computations, probes and certificates.
- `research.collatz`: signed Collatz maps, orbit codes, fibres and cycles.
- `research.collatz_finite_descent`: finite descent analysis.
- `research.syracuse`: accelerated Collatz experiments.

Shared support: `research.experiments`, `research.open_problems`,
`research.conjectures`, `research.literature`, `bt` and `research_engine`.
The scope policy is [data/lab_scope.json](../../data/lab_scope.json).

Independent applications have been deleted, along with their tests and records.
See [Git recovery](../history.md). Do not recreate a legacy namespace as a shim.
