# Coherent fine-scale mixing can coexist with summable fixed-root densities

The [explicit ternary model](../problems/collatz_fibre_mixing.md), 23 September
2026, has positive unit cylinders, exact refinement coherence, conserved mean
and uniform exponentially small refinement errors. Nevertheless, at any chosen
ordinary unit integer its depth-density series is geometric and summable.
Both initial rows (0,1,1) and (0,1,2) are covered by the Lean proof.

**CLOSE** inference from those general properties to fixed-root divergence.
The construction does not satisfy the actual signed inverse recurrence or
Tao's affine stationarity; a checked one-step inequality separates the unit
model from both signed operators. It refutes no actual Collatz coefficient
lower bound. The arithmetic fixed-root and Juggler pressure questions remain
open. Ledger: C-fibre-mixing-pointwise-obstruction.
