# Near-critical periodic weights already force the global minimum-cell rate

The [rate-barrier audit](../problems/collatz_fibre_rate_barrier.md),
23 September 2026, proves that any nonzero periodic subsolution at rate q
gives C_(d+1)(a)>=kappa_r*h(b)*q^d at every positive odd unit root a,
where kappa_r=3/2^(2*3^r)>0. The level and constant are fixed while depth
varies. Hence nonzero tables at rates tending to one imply, for every
t<1, one positive constant c_t with C_(d+1)(a)>=c_t*t^d uniformly in a,d.
For plus this is the uniform subexponential Syracuse-cell lower premise.

around that global rate problem. The near-critical family itself remains
open, and its quantitative root-to-deficit condition is additional. This
is not a refutation of the finite certificates, that family, or actual
fixed-root divergence. A direct lower count at one ordinary integer need
