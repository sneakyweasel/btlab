# Polynomial stationary duals do not extend to arbitrary word length

[Exact classification](../problems/juggler_polynomial_dual.md), 22 September
2026. **CLOSE** direct extension of polynomial periodicity to all longer
smooth word phases. The dual degree is 3^o/(3^o-2^L), integral exactly
when the signed Collatz gap is one. `PolynomialDual.lean` proves the
only positive cases are (o,L)=(1,1),(2,3), with degrees 3 and 9.
Already OO gives 9/5, and repeating OOE gives 81/17. The exceptional
ninth-degree complete cancellation supplies no estimate for the actual
OOE parity-selected sum. This does not close the existing perturbed
cubic method, frequency averaging, or other nonpolynomial analytic
methods. Member: `J-polynomial-dual-unit-gap`.
