# Collatz orbit packing does not bypass Juggler parity counts

The [orbit-packing audit](../problems/juggler_collatz_bridge.md) separates
equal-time injectivity from the exact binomial residue count in the
Collatz argument. Only the first step transfers automatically. For a
hypothetical unbounded Juggler orbit, the five existing certificate
classes give A(X)<=R_5(X)+floor(X^(1/2))+floor(X^(3/4))
+floor(X^(9/16))+2*floor(X^(27/32)). Paper B's written five-step count
then gives upper natural density at most 1/8, not an exclusion or
reciprocal summability. Its independent-review limitation is inherited.

**CLOSE** automatic packing transfer as a bypass of growing-depth
arithmetic. The 2-adic code identifies parity cells but does not bound
their integer-source multiplicities or transport ordinary heights.
At depth five on starts 1,...,32, actual Juggler odd-count multiplicities
are (0,0,17,13,1,1), not the binomial (1,5,10,10,5,1); this only refutes
the exact finite law. Growing-depth live-cell counts remain the open
pressure input. No general impossibility theorem for orbit counting is
claimed, and the earlier valley-composition closure is unchanged.
