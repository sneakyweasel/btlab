# Ancestor generating-function rigidity does not supply harmonic divergence

The [analytic applicability audit](../problems/collatz_ancestor_generating_audit.md),
23 September 2026, distinguishes the known Hardy fixed-point theorem from
the Bergman norm encoding reciprocal mass. At a nonperiodic root a, the
ancestor indicator b satisfies b-b o T=delta_a, not a homogeneous fixed-point
equation. Lean checks that it is the least nonnegative solution and that
a nonnegative source solution with finite weighted square sum exists
exactly when the weighted ancestor mass is finite.

**CLOSE** automatic transfer from the published analytic results. Bergman
expansiveness fails even on the unit monomials z^7 for plus and z^47 for
minus. The actual ancestor ray of 3 has reciprocal mass 2/3 for both signs,
while Bell-Lagarias gives its generating function a natural boundary. That
nonunit example does not refute the unit-root lower target. The latter,
and Juggler's required pressure estimate, remain open. No impossibility
claim for a genuinely new analytic estimate is made.
