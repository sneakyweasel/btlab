# The Guo--Guo--Jing bilinear bound cannot directly cross the density line

**22 September 2026.** Theorem 1.1 of arXiv:2510.20562v1 has two
unavoidable terms whose maximum is at least X^(4/5), for every normalized
exponent pair and factorization X=MN with M,N>=1. At the exported
two-monomial amplitude Delta asymptotic to X^(9/4), the fourth term
raises the optimized floor to X^(5/6), even granting (0,1/2).
The required exponent is below 2/3. See the exact calculation in
[the external note](../theory/exponent_pair_two_monomial.md).

**CLOSE** direct use of this formula by tuning factor lengths or exponent
pairs. These floors concern its upper-bound expression, not the actual
sum. A different bilinear argument with extra structure is not excluded;
no representation of growing-depth Juggler pressure is supplied here.
