## Results

### 1. The complete circular log-gap equation

Let a primitive actual cubic cycle have sorted states \(c_0,\ldots,c_{L-1}\),
minimum \(m>1\), cutoff \(o\), and \(e=L-o\), with
\(\gcd(e,L)=1\). Put

\[
T=\log3,\quad z_i=\log\log c_i,\quad
\sigma(i)=i+e\pmod L.
\]

Close the ordered coordinates into a circle of circumference \(T\):

\[
g_i=z_{i+1}-z_i\ (i<L-1),\qquad
g_{L-1}=T+z_0-z_{L-1}.
\]

All \(g_i>0\), and \(\sum g_i=T\), because \(M<m^3\).
Let \(a_O=\log(3/2)\), \(a_E=-\log2\), and define the initialized
defects

\[
\delta_i=z_i+a_{b_i}-z_{\sigma(i)}\ge0.
\]

Here \(b_i=O\) for \(i<o\), and \(b_i=E\) otherwise. Since the two
branch shifts differ by \(T\), subtraction gives, including the cut
and wrap,

\[
g_{\sigma(i)}-g_i=\delta_i-\delta_{i+1}.
\tag{CW1}
\]

Indices on \(g,\delta\) are circular. At the cut the output gap is
the positive wrap gap; at the wrap the output is the original E/O image
seam. Thus no signed or fictitious ordinary gap is introduced.
The defect sum is

\[
\sum_i\delta_i=\Lambda:=o\log3-L\log2>0.
\tag{CW2}
\]

### 2. A genuine entropy sign, but one that is purely geometric

Write \(d_i=\delta_{i+1}-\delta_i=g_i-g_{\sigma(i)}\). Then

\[
\sum_i\frac{d_i}{g_i}
=L-\sum_i\frac{g_{\sigma(i)}}{g_i}\le0.
\tag{CW3}
\]

Indeed the positive ratios have product one, so arithmetic mean is at
least geometric mean. Equality requires every ratio to be one. The
transitivity of \(\sigma\) then requires all gaps to be equal.

There is an exact convex-potential formulation. For any differentiable
convex \(\Phi:(0,\infty)\to\mathbb R\), define

\[
B_\Phi(a,b)=\Phi(a)-\Phi(b)-\Phi'(b)(a-b)\ge0.
\]

Permutation invariance of the sum of \(\Phi(g_i)\) gives

\[
\sum_i\Phi'(g_i)d_i
=\sum_i B_\Phi(g_{\sigma(i)},g_i)\ge0.
\tag{CW4}
\]

For strictly convex \(\Phi\), equality again requires uniform gaps.
Taking \(\Phi=-\log\) gives the negative of (CW3). Taking
\(\Phi(x)=x\log x\) gives an entropy expression with weights
\(1+\log g_i\); the constant part vanishes because \(\sum d_i=0\).

For example, if \(g_{\min}\le g_i\le g_{\max}\), the second derivative
of \(-\log x\) gives the quantitative bounds

\[
\frac{\sum_i d_i^2}{2g_{\max}^2}
\le-\sum_i\frac{d_i}{g_i}
\le\frac{\sum_i d_i^2}{2g_{\min}^2}.
\tag{CW5}
\]

These signs and strictness statements are valid and useful for auditing
a proposed weighted identity. But their proofs use only a positive
vector and a permutation. They hold before any branch power, integer
state, parity, or remainder face is supplied. They therefore cannot
by themselves distinguish a Juggler cycle from a positive-vector model.
Section 4 strengthens this limitation to models satisfying every real
square-cell inequality.

### 3. Positive cyclic Green kernels still produce signed coefficients

Let \((Pf)_i=f_{\sigma(i)}\), \((Af)_i=f_{i+1}\), and let \(\Pi\)
project onto constant vectors by taking the mean. Equation (CW1) is

\[
(I-P)g=(A-I)\delta.
\]

The explicit nonnegative cyclic Green kernel

\[
G=\frac1L\sum_{k=0}^{L-1}(L-1-k)P^k
\]

satisfies

\[
(I-P)G=G(I-P)=I-\Pi.
\]

This follows by subtracting neighboring coefficients, using \(P^L=I\)
and \(L\Pi=\sum_{k=0}^{L-1}P^k\). Consequently

\[
g-\frac TL\mathbf1=G(A-I)\delta.
\tag{CW6}
\]

Although \(G\) is entrywise nonnegative, each row of \(G(A-I)\) has
coefficient sum zero. Every nonzero such row therefore has coefficients
of both signs. The operator is nonzero and invertible between the
zero-mean subspaces: both \(I-P\) and \(A-I\) have only constants in
their kernels. Because the operators commute with the cyclic adjacent
shift, their rows are cyclic translates, so every row is nonzero for
\(L>1\).

Adding a constant projection to make another Green normalization more
positive changes nothing: \(\Pi(A-I)=0\). Thus positivity of the Green
kernel does not convert adjacent remainder differences into a positive
linear charge. This is an algebraic obstruction to that particular
inference, not a prohibition on all state-dependent weighted arguments.

### 4. Strict real square-cell models at every prescribed large minimum

**Theorem.** For every prescribed real \(m>1\), there exist coprime
positive integers \(e,L\), with \(e<o=L-e<2e\), and sorted real states
\(c_0=m<\cdots<c_{L-1}<m^3\), such that

\[
c_i<m^2\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad i<o,
\]

and, with \(n_i=3\) below the cut and \(n_i=1\) above it,

\[
c_{\sigma(i)}^2<c_i^{n_i}<(c_{\sigma(i)}+1)^2
\quad\text{for every }i.
\tag{CW7}
\]

Their log-log gaps are uniform and their initialized log-log defects
are a common positive constant. Thus even a zero entropy value is
compatible with all strict *real* square-cell faces at arbitrary scale.

**Proof.** Put

\[
\theta=\frac{\log(3/2)}{\log3},\qquad
\eta_m=\log\!\left(\frac{\log(m^3+1)}{\log(m^3)}\right)>0.
\]

One has \(1/3<\theta<1/2\). Also \(\theta\) is irrational: a rational
equality \(\theta=p/q\) would give \(3^{q-p}=2^q\), contradicting
unique prime factorization for positive \(q\).

Choose an integer \(N\) sufficiently large that
\(r=\lfloor N\theta\rfloor/N>1/3\) and \(T/N<\eta_m\).
Reduce \(r=e/L\) to lowest terms. Then

\[
\frac13<\frac eL<\theta<\frac12,\qquad
0<\theta-\frac eL<\frac1N\le\frac1L.
\tag{CW8}
\]

Set

\[
c_i=m^{3^{i/L}},\qquad
\bar\delta=\log(3/2)-\frac{eT}{L}.
\]

These states are strictly ordered and in \([m,m^3)\). Their logarithmic
coordinates are \(z_i=\log\log m+iT/L\). Since
\(\log2/T=1-\theta\), (CW8) gives

\[
\frac{o-1}{L}<1-\theta<\frac oL,
\]

which proves the exact threshold cut. Rank addition gives
\(z_{\sigma(i)}=z_i+a_{b_i}-\bar\delta\), so all defects equal
\(\bar\delta>0\), and \(\Lambda=L\bar\delta\).

The function

\[
\eta(c)=\log\!\left(\frac{\log(c+1)}{\log c}\right)
\]

is strictly decreasing for \(c>1\), because
\(\eta'(c)=1/[(c+1)\log(c+1)]-1/[c\log c]<0\).
Thus

\[
0<\bar\delta<T/N<\eta_m<\eta(c_{\sigma(i)}).
\]

The initialized defect relation is equivalent to
\(c_i^{n_i}=c_{\sigma(i)}^{2\exp(\bar\delta)}\). The last displayed
bound is exactly (CW7), after taking logarithms. This proves the theorem.

The initialized real square remainders
\(R_i=c_i^{n_i}-c_{\sigma(i)}^2\) consequently satisfy
\(0<R_i<2c_{\sigma(i)}+1\). They are not independent fictitious phases.
However the output coordinates need not be integers, so (CW7) is a
relaxation of floor equality, not an actual threshold-map orbit.

### 5. Every centered defect direction occurs in that strict relaxation

Fix one model from Section 4. For a real vector \(u\) with \(u_0=0\),
perturb \(z_i\) to \(z_i+t u_i\). The initialized defects change exactly
by

\[
\delta_i(t)=\bar\delta+t(u_i-u_{\sigma(i)}).
\tag{CW9}
\]

The map

\[
\{u:u_0=0\}\longrightarrow\{d:\sum_i d_i=0\},
\qquad u\longmapsto u-u\circ\sigma
\]

is a linear bijection. To prove it constructively, start at \(u_0=0\)
and set
\(u_{\sigma^k(0)}=-\sum_{j<k}d_{\sigma^j(0)}\).
The zero sum closes this assignment after \(L\) steps; transitivity
visits every index and gives uniqueness.

All order, cut, wrap, lower-cell, and upper-cell inequalities of the
baseline are strict. There are finitely many of them and their sides
are continuous functions of \(z\). Hence, for each direction \(u\),
both signs of sufficiently small nonzero \(t\) preserve every condition
in (CW7), the fixed minimum \(m\), the rank permutation, and positivity
of all gaps and defects. The defect sum remains \(\Lambda\).

**Fixed-weight consequence.** For any fixed nonconstant real weights
\(w_i\), put \(w_i^0=w_i-\overline w\). Use (CW9) with defect
direction \(d=w^0\). Then

\[
\sum_i w_i\bigl(\delta_i(t)-\Lambda/L\bigr)
=t\sum_i(w_i^0)^2,
\tag{CW10}
\]

which takes both signs in strict closed real-cell configurations.
In particular this holds for positive nonconstant fixed weights.

The same conclusion holds for a full-circle fixed weighted adjacent
defect sum. Since

\[
\sum_i w_i(\delta_{i+1}-\delta_i)
=\sum_i(w_{i-1}-w_i)\delta_i,
\]

choose the nonzero zero-mean defect direction
\(d_i=w_{i-1}-w_i\). The resulting expression is
\(t\sum_i(w_{i-1}-w_i)^2\), again of either sign.

These conclusions concern weights fixed before the perturbation. They
do not contradict (CW3)–(CW5): inverse-gap and convex-gradient weights
change with the state, and their sign is a geometric second-order
constraint. Any nonzero perturbation above makes the gaps nonuniform,
so (CW3) is strictly negative for **both** perturbation signs.

**Continuous common-edge weights.** There is a stronger statement on
the two common-edge paths
\(I=\{0,\ldots,o-2\}\cup\{o,\ldots,L-2\}\). Suppose each
\(w_i(z)\), \(i\in I\), is continuous near the baseline, and the
baseline weights are not all zero. Define

\[
S(z)=\sum_{i\in I}w_i(z)(\delta_{i+1}-\delta_i),\qquad
\beta_j=\mathbf1_{j-1\in I}w_{j-1}(z^0)
          -\mathbf1_{j\in I}w_j(z^0).
\]

Then \(\sum_j\beta_j=0\), and \(\beta\ne0\). Indeed, if this
boundary vanished, the first vertex of either path would force its
first edge weight to vanish; successive vertices would force every
weight on that path to vanish. This contradicts the hypothesis.

Choose the defect direction \(d=\beta\) in (CW9). Discrete summation
by parts and continuity give

\[
S(z^0+t u)=t\sum_j\beta_j^2+o(t).
\tag{CW10a}
\]

Thus the common-edge weighted adjacent-defect sum takes both signs
in arbitrarily nearby strict real-cell models. The weights may vary
with the states; differentiability is unnecessary. This proof and its
incidence argument were cross-checked by a separate AI agent. It still does not assert a state-dependent sign reversal on the
full circle: there a nonzero constant edge flow has zero boundary,
which is precisely why the entropy examples escape this argument.


### 6. The exact integer parity contribution

Return to hypothetical actual integer states, with \(e<o\), and put
\[
R_i=c_i^{n_i}-c_{\sigma(i)}^2,\qquad a=o-e,
\]
where \(n_i=3\) below rank \(o\), one above it.
Let \(p_i=c_i\bmod2\), and \(b_i=1\) for \(i<o\), zero otherwise.
Reduction modulo two gives \(R_i\bmod2=p_i\mathbin{\mathrm{XOR}}p_{\sigma(i)}\).
The required differences \(b_i\mathbin{\mathrm{XOR}}b_{\sigma(i)}\) are
zero for \(i<a\) and one for \(i\ge a\). Matching this pattern makes
\(p_i\mathbin{\mathrm{XOR}}b_i\) invariant under \(\sigma\); primitivity
makes it constant, and an odd minimum makes it zero.
Conversely, all correct guards give the pattern. Without the seed there
are two global choices; without primitivity there is one per rank orbit.
Thus the pattern plus the odd minimum is exactly the full guard test in
other coordinates, not a compression.

Write \(R_i=\epsilon_i+2K_i\), \(K_i\ge0\) integral, with
\(\epsilon_i=0\) before \(a\) and one from \(a\) onward. For common edges
\(I=\{0,\ldots,L-2\}\setminus\{o-1\}\), let \(\beta\) be the incidence
vector of weights \(w_i\): each edge contributes \(-w_i\) at its source,
\(+w_i\) at its target. The unique parity jump is at \(a-1\in I\), so
\[
\boxed{\sum_{i\in I}w_i(R_{i+1}-R_i)
=w_{a-1}+2\sum_i\beta_iK_i.} \tag{CW11}
\]
For positive weights nonincreasing along each common path, with \(e\ge2\),
the only negative coefficients are \(\beta_0=-w_0\) and \(\beta_o=-w_o\).
The remaining coefficients are adjacent weight differences or positive
terminal weights. No monotonicity of the actual downstream weights is
assumed to have been proved.
The signed carry sum needs an independent estimate from the joint
initialized integer cells. Equation (CW11) provides no such estimate.

### 7. Scope of the obstruction

Constant logarithmic defects do not make raw square remainders constant:
at the model of Result 4,
\[
R_i=c_{\sigma(i)}^{2e^{\bar\delta}}-c_{\sigma(i)}^2.
\]
Consequently the two-sign theorem for normalized defects cannot simply be
copied to an arbitrary unnormalized \(\sum w_i(R_{i+1}-R_i)\).
Any application must retain the target-dependent normalization factors.

The models do not retain integer packing either. For fixed \(m\),
\[
c_1-m=m^{3^{1/L}}-m\sim m\log m\,\log3/L.
\]
It eventually falls below two, whereas distinct odd cycle states have gap
at least two. No claim is made that these configurations satisfy that
condition, the DC/LR height strips, or the full guarded return constraints.
Nor is the unavailable terminal exponent premise \(\mu<1\) assumed.

### 8. Complete positive integer gap transport

**Theorem (J-cycle-integer-weight-refinements).** Results 8–16 are the
authorized integer continuation of this gate. Assume the actual primitive
cubic-cycle geometry, \(m\ge3\), \(L>2\), and \(e<o\). They give exact
transport and distributional restrictions. They do not supply a strict
contraction or a new numerical cycle bound.

Keep \(h=c_{o-1}\), \(s=c_o\), \(t=c_{e-1}=E(M)\), \(q=c_e=O(m)\),
and \(R_i=c_i^{n_i}-c_{\sigma(i)}^2\), with \(n_i=3\) below o and 1
above it. Distinguish the following integer gaps from the logarithmic
gaps \(g_i\) in Result 1:
\[
\ell_i=c_{i+1}-c_i\quad(i<L-1),\qquad \ell_{L-1}=m^3-M.
\tag{CW12}
\]
They are positive, sum to \(m^3-m\), and are even except at the cut
\(o-1\) and wrap \(L-1\), where they are odd. In particular
\(\ell_0\ge2\). The lifted endpoint \(m^3\) is not an extra cycle state.

There are positive integers \(N_i,B_i\) and integer corrections \(r_i\)
such that every rank transition satisfies
\[
\boxed{B_i\ell_{\sigma(i)}=N_i\ell_i-r_i.}
\tag{CW13}
\]
On a common branch edge \(i\ne o-1,L-1\), use
\[
N_i=S_{n_i}(c_i,c_{i+1}),\quad
B_i=c_{\sigma(i)}+c_{\sigma(i+1)},\quad r_i=R_{i+1}-R_i,
\]
where \(S_1=1\) and \(S_3(x,y)=x^2+xy+y^2\). Subtracting the two
actual square cells proves (CW13).

At the cut, put \(Q_s=s^2+sm^2+m^4\) and use
\[
N_{o-1}=h^2+hs+s^2,\quad B_{o-1}=m^3+M,\quad
r_{o-1}=Q_sR_o-R_{o-1}.
\tag{CW14}
\]
Indeed \(s=m^2+R_o\), so \(s^3-m^6=Q_sR_o\), while
\(R_{o-1}=h^3-M^2\). Hence
\[
(m^3+M)(m^3-M)=s^3-h^3-Q_sR_o+R_{o-1}.
\]
This is an algebraic lift of the actual E-source cell at s; it never
treats \(O(s)\) as a genuine odd step from even s.

At the wrap, use
\[
N_{L-1}=1,\quad B_{L-1}=q+t,\quad r_{L-1}=R_0-R_{L-1},
\tag{CW15}
\]
because \(q^2-t^2=m^3-M-(R_0-R_{L-1})\). These two transitions
replace the two mixed boundaries in the terminal factorization. The
common P and Q paths cover every remaining rank edge exactly once,
as proved in the [terminal dossier](juggler_cycle_terminal_joint_cells.md).

Let \(j_k=\sigma^k(0)\), \(0\le k<L\), and set
\[
A_i=N_i/B_i,\quad \mathcal P=\prod_i A_i,\quad
W_{j_k}=\frac1{B_{j_k}}\prod_{\nu=k+1}^{L-1}A_{j_\nu}>0.
\tag{CW16}
\]
Iterating (CW13) through all L transitions gives
\[
\boxed{\mathcal F:=\sum_i W_ir_i=(\mathcal P-1)\ell_0.}
\tag{CW17}
\]
The weights use the actual states and all downstream transitions.
Neither their product nor the terminal secants are replaced by the
product of ideal exponents.

If \(j=\sigma(i)\ne0\) is the next edge in this unrolled order, then
\[
\frac{W_i}{W_j}=\frac{N_j}{B_i}
=\frac{S_{n_j}(x_j,y_j)}{x_j+y_j},
\tag{CW18}
\]
where \((x_j,y_j)\) is its source pair, with \((M,m^3)\) at the wrap
and the cubic numerator at the cut. Thus the weights fall across a
following O transition and rise across a following E transition.
At the closing transition back to edge zero there is an additional
factor \(1/\mathcal P\). This chronological rule does not prove
monotonicity on the two paths in sorted-rank order used by (CW11).

### 9. Positive parity contribution, signed carries and nonvanishing

Collect the original remainders in (CW17). With circular rank indices,
define
\[
\Gamma_j=W_{j-1}-W_j\quad(j\ne o),\qquad
\Gamma_o=Q_sW_{o-1}-W_o.
\]
Then
\[
\mathcal F=\sum_j\Gamma_jR_j,\qquad
\sum_j\Gamma_j=(Q_s-1)W_{o-1}>0.
\tag{CW19}
\]
The extra term is the cubic lift of the minimum's E-source remainder;
these coefficients are not the zero-sum incidence vector of a plain
circular difference.

Write \(a=o-e\), \(R_j=\epsilon_j+2K_j\), where
\(\epsilon_j=0\) before a and 1 thereafter. The exact parity and cell
bounds give \(0\le K_j\le c_{\sigma(j)}-\epsilon_j\). Consequently
\[
\mathcal F=\mathcal B+2\sum_j\Gamma_jK_j,\qquad
\mathcal B=W_{a-1}-W_{L-1}+(Q_s-1)W_{o-1}.
\]
The cut is followed immediately by the wrap in the unrolled orbit, so
\(W_{L-1}=(m^3+M)W_{o-1}\). Therefore
\[
\boxed{\mathcal B=
W_{a-1}+(Q_s-1-m^3-M)W_{o-1}>0.}
\tag{CW20}
\]
For the sign use \(s\ge m^2+1\), hence
\(Q_s\ge3m^4+3m^2+1\), and \(M<m^3\).
This positive compulsory part does not determine the full sum's sign.

For fixed actual weights the sharp bound from the separate carry boxes is
\[
\mathcal B+2\sum_{\Gamma_j<0}\Gamma_j(c_{\sigma(j)}-\epsilon_j)
\le\mathcal F\le
\mathcal B+2\sum_{\Gamma_j>0}\Gamma_j(c_{\sigma(j)}-\epsilon_j).
\tag{CW21}
\]
It is sharp for those independent coordinate boxes, not for the
coupled feasible carries of a cycle. No sign for its lower expression,
or comparison forcing strict contraction, has been proved here.
The equivalent exact level-set identity is
\[
\sum_j\Gamma_jK_j
=\sum_{k\ge1}\ \sum_{j:K_j\ge k}\Gamma_j.
\tag{CW22}
\]
Thus a useful sign would require control of the weighted boundaries of
the actual carry-level sets. Source order alone does not supply that
control. The earlier local E faces show why monotonicity of carries
cannot be inferred merely from increasing endpoints; they do not
refute spatial weight monotonicity for a complete actual cycle.

There is nevertheless a strict integer fact. Put
\(\mathcal N=\prod_iN_i\), \(\mathcal D=\prod_iB_i\).
Every numerator is odd: on common O edges both sources are odd, at the
cut one is odd and one even, and every other numerator is 1. Each
denominator is the endpoint sum of the image gap and has its parity.
Exactly two image gaps are odd, so exactly two denominators are odd.
For \(L>2\),
\[
\mathcal N\text{ is odd},\quad 2^{L-2}\mid\mathcal D,\quad
\mathcal N-\mathcal D\text{ is odd and nonzero}.
\]
It follows that
\[
\boxed{\mathcal P\ne1,\qquad
|\mathcal F|=\frac{|\mathcal N-\mathcal D|\ell_0}{\mathcal D}
\ge\frac{\ell_0}{\mathcal D}\ge\frac2{\mathcal D}>0.}
\tag{CW23}
\]
Clearing denominators explicitly, let
\[
Z_k=\left(\prod_{\nu<k}B_{j_\nu}\right)
    \left(\prod_{\nu>k}N_{j_\nu}\right)\in\mathbb Z_{>0}.
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{\sum_k Z_kr_{j_k}=(\mathcal N-\mathcal D)\ell_0,\qquad
\nu_2\left(\sum_k Z_kr_{j_k}\right)=\nu_2(\ell_0).}
\tag{CW24}
\]
For a negative integer the valuation means that of its absolute value.
These are nonvanishing and exact valuation consequences of the
initialized cells and parity. They do not decide the sign of
\(\mathcal N-\mathcal D\). Their nonzero force is fully compatible
with closure (CW17); it is not an extra contradictory congruence.
The bound is on a denominator-product scale,
\((2m)^L<\mathcal D<(2m^3)^L\), and supplies no new period or height
comparison.

### 10. Integer defects cannot be uniform, but the entropy comparison is weak

Keep the logarithmic \(g_i,\delta_i\) of Result 1, and define
\[
\eta(y)=\log\log(y+1)-\log\log y
=\int_y^{y+1}\frac{du}{u\log u},\qquad y>1.
\]
It is positive and decreasing. An actual floor edge with target y has
\(\delta<\eta(y)\). An even-to-odd edge \(v\to y\) has
\(v\ge y^2+1\), hence \(\delta\ge\eta(y^2)\).
Thus on every actual OE passage \(x\to v\to y\), with odd y,
\[
\boxed{\delta_E-\delta_O>
D(y):=\eta(y^2)-\eta(y^2+1)>0.}
\tag{CW25}
\]
Indeed \(\delta_O<\eta(v)\le\eta(y^2+1)\).
The exact matched pairs in cubic ranks are
\(c_{o-e+j}\to c_{o+j}\to c_j\), \(0\le j<e\). Therefore
\[
\sum_{j=0}^{e-1}(\delta_{o+j}-\delta_{o-e+j})
>\sum_{j=0}^{e-1}D(c_j)>0.
\tag{CW26}
\]
This fixed-rank signed restriction uses the integer guards and fails
in the earlier constant-defect real relaxation.

There is also a stronger endpoint contrast. Since \(M\ge s\ge m^2+1\),
\[
C:=\eta(m^2)-\eta(M)>0,\qquad
\boxed{g_{o-1}-g_{L-1}=\delta_o-\delta_{o-1}>C.}
\tag{CW27}
\]
The two inequalities on the defects use the genuine edges \(s\to m\)
and \(h\to M\). Thus both defects and logarithmic gaps are nonuniform.
This is a consequence of the already available extrema cells, not a
new height theorem.

For completeness, at \(m\ge64\) one has \(C>1/(10m^2\log m)\).
The first image \(q=O(m)\) must be odd, since otherwise \(E(q)<m\).
Hence \(M\ge O(q)\). Now \(q\ge(7/8)m^{3/2}\), and
\[
q^3\ge(343/512)m^{9/2}\ge(343/64)m^4>4m^4,
\]
so \(M\ge2m^2\). Monotonicity of \(1/(u\log u)\) gives
\[
C>\frac1{(m^2+1)\log(m^2+1)}
-\frac1{2m^2\log(2m^2)}
\ge\frac3{10m^2\log(2m^2)}
\ge\frac1{10m^2\log m}.
\tag{CW28}
\]
Here \(m^2+1\le(5/4)m^2\) and \(\log(2m^2)\le3\log m\).

Let \(d_i=\delta_{i+1}-\delta_i\) and
\(\mathcal E=\sum_i g_{\sigma(i)}/g_i-L\). Since
\(d_{o-1}>C\) and \(\sum_i d_i=0\), Cauchy--Schwarz gives
\(\sum_i d_i^2>LC^2/(L-1)\). The existing convexity estimate (CW5) yields
\[
\mathcal E>\frac{LC^2}{2(L-1)g_{\max}^2}>0.
\tag{CW29}
\]
Similarly \(\sum_i(\delta_i-\Lambda/L)^2>C^2/2\).
These are quantitative integer refinements of the earlier geometric
nonnegativity, with written proofs rather than numerical evidence.

They do not close the cycle. Nonnegative defects satisfy
\[
\sum_i d_i^2\le2\Lambda^2,\qquad
\sum_i(\delta_i-\Lambda/L)^2\le\frac{L-1}{L}\Lambda^2.
\]
Either energy comparison gives only
\[
\Lambda>\sqrt{\frac{L}{2(L-1)}}\,C.
\tag{CW30}
\]
Its right side is smaller than \(\eta(m^2)=b(m)\), the already
compulsory last-even loss. Likewise \(D(y)<b(y)\), so the scalar
consequence of (CW26) is weaker than the existing parity-switch
budget. The distributional restrictions remain valid; no independent
upper estimate on the signed carry sum or entropy has been obtained.

### 11. Nested carry levels attain exactly the coordinate-box bound

**Authorized continuation of J-cycle-integer-weight-refinements.**
Results 11–13 test whether carry-level geometry improves (CW21).
The coefficients are frozen when optimizing a containing box. This
does not freeze the weights of a different actual cycle.

Put \(U_i=c_{\sigma(i)}-\epsilon_i\), and reorder these caps by target
rank \(t=\sigma(i)\). Then
\[
\widetilde U_t=
\begin{cases}
c_t-1,&0\le t<e,\\
c_t,&e\le t<o,\\
c_t-1,&o\le t<L.
\end{cases}
\tag{CW31}
\]
These caps strictly increase. Same-parity gaps are at least two; at
\(e-1\) the difference gains one. At the cut it is \(s-h-1\ge2\),
because \(h\le m^2-2\) and \(s\ge m^2+1\). Thus
\(T_k=\{i:U_i\ge k\}\) is a suffix in target order and a circular
interval in source order. This is the shape of the allowed envelope,
not necessarily of the occupied carry-level set.

For any finite index set, nonnegative integer caps \(U_i\), and real
coefficients \(\Gamma_i\), integer vectors \(0\le K_i\le U_i\) are
in bijection with nested sets
\[
S_{k+1}\subseteq S_k\subseteq T_k.
\]
The forward map is \(S_k=\{i:K_i\ge k\}\); the inverse counts the
levels containing each index. Nestedness makes those levels an initial
segment, and the envelope bounds its length by \(U_i\). Therefore
\[
\boxed{\min\sum_{k\ge1}\Gamma(S_k)
=\sum_{\Gamma_i<0}\Gamma_iU_i,\qquad
\Gamma(S)=\sum_{i\in S}\Gamma_i.}
\tag{CW32}
\]
The minimum is attained by the nested sets
\(S_k=T_k\cap\{i:\Gamma_i<0\}\). The maximum uses positive coefficients.
This remains true for the circular-interval envelopes in (CW31):
their intersection with the negative-coefficient set need not be an
interval. Additional integer lower clamps \(0\le L_i\le U_i\), with
\(L_i\le K_i\), give the
same coordinate minimum, with the extra term
\(\sum_{\Gamma_i>0}\Gamma_iL_i\). Nesting alone adds no inequality.

Exact E cells and their source order do not repair this relaxation.
Fix any increasing positive odd integer targets \(y_0<\cdots<y_{r-1}\).
Every integer choice \(0\le k_j\le y_j-1\) is realized by
\[
\boxed{x_j=y_j^2+1+2k_j\xrightarrow E y_j.}
\tag{CW33}
\]
All sources are even and satisfy
\(y_j^2<x_j\le(y_j+1)^2-2\). Even the largest source for \(y_j\)
is below the smallest source for \(y_{j+1}\), since their difference
is at least \(2y_j+6>0\). Hence every allowed carry vector is realized
by ordered genuine E cells when the sources are free. Additional
individual source intervals only clamp their corresponding carries.

This assertion does not supply the odd predecessors, shared cycle
coordinates, global height anchors, or the same recomputed weights.
For the actual cycle, with \(a=o-e\), the E cells must also satisfy
\[
c_{a+j}^3=(c_j^2+1+2K_{o+j})^2+1+2K_{a+j},
\qquad 0\le j<e.
\tag{CW34}
\]
Some predecessors are themselves other targets in the same list.
Moreover, a fixed positive integer O target y has at most the one source
\(\lceil y^{2/3}\rceil\), which must be odd and satisfy
\(x^3<(y+1)^2\). Its carry is then fixed. These cube-lattice
conditions are stronger than the envelopes and remain in the actual
problem. The free E construction realizes no arbitrary O carries
and no complete cycle.

### 12. The OE contrast adds no constraint to its initialized local boxes

Write an actual OE passage as \(x\to v\to y\), with \(y\ge3\) odd,
\[
v=y^2+1+2l,\quad 0\le l\le y-1,\qquad
x^3=v^2+1+2k,\quad 0\le k\le v-1.
\]
Retaining both logarithmic normalizations gives
\[
\delta_E-\delta_O
=\log\frac{(\log v)^2}{\log y\,\log(v^2+1+2k)}.
\tag{CW35}
\]
This is not the raw carry difference \(l-k\).

For \(D(y)=\eta(y^2)-\eta(y^2+1)\), the proved comparison (CW25)
is equivalent to
\[
1+2k<B_D(y,v):=
\exp\!\left(e^{-D(y)}\frac{(\log v)^2}{\log y}\right)-v^2.
\]
Put \(v_0=y^2+1\). Direct substitution gives
\(B_D(y,v_0)=2v_0+1\). At \(l=0\) the comparison is therefore
exactly \(k<v\), or the existing integer cap \(k\le v-1\).

For \(v>v_0\), the function \((\log v)^2/\log(v+1)\) strictly
increases: after clearing positive factors, its derivative has sign
\(2(v+1)\log(v+1)-v\log v>0\). Consequently
\(B_D(y,v)>2v+1\). The existing cap already gives
\(1+2k\le2v-1\), so the comparison is weaker there.

Thus every pair in these initialized local boxes satisfies (CW25).
Appending it or its nonnegative combinations removes no points from
this relaxation and cannot improve an objective's exact minimum on
that same domain. Keeping v fixed while independently varying l
would violate the stated shared-state equation.
The boxes omit the odd-cube requirement and repeated identifications
around the cycle. This redundancy result does not rule out combining
the valid defect comparison with additional global information.

### 13. Spatial signs require return products of unbounded length

The chronological rule (CW18) has an exact spatial version.
Let \(u\in\{1,\ldots,L-1\}\) satisfy \(eu\equiv1\pmod L\), and put
\(v=L-u\). These are the terminal U and V lengths from the return
factorization. Define the source endpoint sums
\[
V_i=c_i+c_{i+1}\ (i<L-1),\qquad V_{L-1}=M+m^3,\qquad
H_i=N_i/V_i.
\]
Every coefficient in (CW13) has \(B_i=V_{\sigma(i)}\), so
\(\prod_i H_i=\mathcal P\). Let
\(\tau(i)=ui\bmod L\) be the unrolled position of rank i, and set
\(\chi_i=1\) when \(\tau(i)<u\), zero otherwise. Successive use of
(CW18), including its closing factor, gives
\[
\boxed{\frac{W_{i-1}}{W_i}
=\mathcal P^{-\chi_i}
\prod_{r=1}^{u}H_{\sigma^r(i-1)}.}
\tag{CW36}
\]
The path has u steps because \(\sigma^u(i-1)=i\). It crosses edge
zero exactly when \(\chi_i=1\); then its product divided by
\(\mathcal P\) is the reciprocal product on the complementary
v-step path.

For \(i\ne o\), the condition \(\Gamma_i\ge0\) is exactly that
the product in (CW36) is at least \(\mathcal P^{\chi_i}\).
For \(i=o\), multiply that product by \(Q_s\). These are conditions
on initialized coefficients over long return blocks. The blocks can
cross the lifted boundaries; they cannot all be treated as one common
prescribed word. Fixed short-word contractions do not directly bound
these products or establish spatial monotonicity.

More explicitly, let \(G_U(i)=\prod_{r=0}^{u-1}A_{\sigma^r(i-1)}\)
and \(G_V(i)=\prod_{r=0}^{v-1}A_{\sigma^r(i)}\).
Their product is \(\mathcal P\), and (CW36) becomes
\[
\frac{W_{i-1}}{W_i}
=\frac{N_i}{N_{i-1}}
\begin{cases}
G_U(i),&\chi_i=0,\\
1/G_V(i),&\chi_i=1.
\end{cases}
\]
The corresponding actual gap relations are
\(\ell_i=G_U(i)\ell_{i-1}-F_U(i)\) and
\(\ell_{i-1}=G_V(i)\ell_i-F_V(i)\), with the transported signed
corrections retained. Bounds on actual output gaps do not by themselves
bound these raw products after those corrections are discarded.
This is the precise unresolved paired-error dependency.

The need for unbounded depth is precise and does not assume a bound
on \(L\Lambda\). Along any hypothetical sequence of actual cubic
cycles with \(m\to\infty\), the floor-cell estimate gives
\[
0<\frac{\Lambda}{L}<\eta(m)\longrightarrow0,\qquad
\frac eL\longrightarrow
\theta:=\frac{\log(3/2)}{\log3},
\tag{CW37}
\]
where \(\theta\) is irrational by unique prime factorization.
Hence \(L\to\infty\). Write \(eu=kL+1\). If u were bounded,
a subsequence would have fixed u and k, forcing \(e/L\to k/u\),
a contradiction. Likewise \(ev=(e-k)L-1\) rules out bounded v.
Thus both terminal lengths tend to infinity.

This is a conditional depth statement, not existence of such cycles.
It identifies the missing type of estimate: control of signed paired
errors uniformly through the return induction, including its complete
cyclic boundary placement. The carry-level continuation obtained
no estimate sufficient to sign the spatial weights or bound the flux.
Results 14–16 below test the subsequent uniform signed-return route.

### 14. Signed composition retains the actual image pair

Let I=(x,y), with x<y, be two actual selected sources that follow a
common guarded word W and have distinct ordered outputs W[I]. Fix
the affine normalization from the initialized square cells in (CW13):
\[
d(W[I])=G_W(I)d(I)-E_W(I),\qquad d(I)=y-x.
\]
The same composition rule applies to the lifted cut and wrap records.
For chronological UV, let I'=U[I]. Substitution gives exactly
\[
\boxed{G_{UV}(I)=G_V(I')G_U(I),\qquad
E_{UV}(I)=G_V(I')E_U(I)+E_V(I').}
\tag{CW38}
\]
This rule is associative with the actual pairs transported at every
stage. The domain also transports: the UV guard at x is the U guard
at x together with the V guard at F_U(x), and likewise at y. Both
Euclidean substitutions restrict their composite branch to sources
whose U images lie in V's old domain. They do not reinitialize V at I.

For the different, full-word ideal-power normalization, write
\[
a_W(I)=\frac{y^{p_W}-x^{p_W}}{y-x},\qquad
e_W(x)=x^{p_W}-F_W(x),\qquad
\Delta_W(I)=e_W(y)-e_W(x).
\]
Let I*=(x^{p_U},y^{p_U}). Comparing the exact final gaps yields
\[
\boxed{\Delta_{UV}(I)=a_V(I')\Delta_U(I)+\Delta_V(I')+
\mathcal C_{U,V}(I),}
\]
\[
\mathcal C_{U,V}(I)=
\bigl(a_V(I*)-a_V(I')\bigr)a_U(I)d(I).
\tag{CW39}
\]
Every actual endpoint is at most its ideal endpoint. The power secant
\(a_p(A,B)=p\int_0^1((1-t)A+tB)^{p-1}\,dt\) decreases in both
endpoints for p<1 and increases for p>1. Consequently the calibration
in (CW39) is nonpositive for p_V<1, zero for p_V=1, and nonnegative
for p_V>1. In particular, a concave suffix need not preserve favorable
component signs in this normalization. No bound p_V<1 is assumed for
the terminal V.

The input gap and its signed change do not determine the absolute
image location needed by (CW38) or (CW39). For example the root secant
on (A,A+d) is \(1/(\sqrt{A+d}+\sqrt A)\), which varies with A.
This is a dependency of the proposed data, not an impossibility theorem
for every richer cycle-specific invariant.

For the fixed affine normalization, put
\(\xi_W=E_W/(G_Wd)<1\). Equation (CW38) implies
\[
1-\xi_{UV}(I)=(1-\xi_U(I))(1-\xi_V(I')).
\tag{CW40}
\]
Thus the cone \(\xi\ge0\) is preserved *if* both actual component
records belong to it. The identity does not prove that membership.
Likewise \(E_W\ge(G_W-1)d\) is exactly actual nonexpansion; its
slack is d minus the output gap. Such slacks telescope to zero on
a closed pair and cannot create a strict decrease by composition alone.

### 15. The common-state signed bound is uniform in depth

Put \(w_i=z_i-z_0-iT/L\), with the notation of Result 1. The rank
rotation gives
\[
\delta_i=\Lambda/L+w_i-w_{\sigma(i)}.
\]
For an r-step actual arc, define
\(D_r(i)=\sum_{k=0}^{r-1}\delta_{\sigma^k(i)}\). Then
\[
D_r(i)=r\Lambda/L+w_i-w_{\sigma^r(i)}.
\tag{CW41}
\]
When r<=L this is a sum over a subset of the cycle and lies in
[0,Lambda]. For general r, delete complete traversals, each of loss
Lambda. This uses the same initialized defect whenever a state is
visited; return words do not introduce fresh independent remainders.

Compare arcs from adjacent ranks i and i+1. Let S_+ be the source
states exclusive to the upper arc and S_- those exclusive to the
lower arc, after deleting common full traversals. Their intersection
has already canceled, so
\[
\Delta_r(i):=D_r(i+1)-D_r(i)
=\sum_{j\in S_+}\delta_j-\sum_{j\in S_-}\delta_j,
\qquad \boxed{|\Delta_r(i)|\le\Lambda.}
\tag{CW42}
\]
There is no word-length factor. Summing (CW1) also gives
\[
\boxed{g_{\sigma^r(i)}=g_i-\Delta_r(i).}
\tag{CW43}
\]
Both formulas include the positive circular wrap gap. Thus this
depth-uniform signed bound is the existing gap-oscillation information
transported through induction. The positive mean in (CW41) cancels
between equal-length arcs; it supplies no orientation for (CW42).

The compulsory integer charges can be retained explicitly. Set
\(b_j=\eta(c_{\sigma(j)}^2)\) at a parity switch and zero otherwise,
and write \(B(S)=\sum_{j\in S}b_j\), \(R=\Lambda-B(\mathrm{all})\).
The already proved lower faces give \(\delta_j\ge b_j\) and R>=0.
Subtracting them in (CW42) proves
\[
\boxed{B(S_+)-B(S_-)-R\le\Delta_r(i)
\le B(S_+)-B(S_-)+R.}
\tag{CW44}
\]
When both exclusive sets are nonempty, these endpoints are sharp
under only the constraints delta_j>=b_j and total loss Lambda: place
all remaining mass R in one exclusive set or the other. This sharpness
does not preserve the upper cells, odd-cube equations or cycle closure.
If both sets are empty the exact discrepancy is zero. No uniformly
useful terminal sign follows from the recorded charge budget alone.

Absolute uniformity is insufficient for relative contraction. Equations
(CW42)–(CW43) give only
\[
\left|g_{\sigma^r(i)}/g_i-1\right|\le\Lambda/g_i.
\]
Oscillation at most Lambda and total gap T give
\(g_{\min}\ge(T-(L-1)\Lambda)/L\). Hence, only if
\((L-1)\Lambda<T\), the resulting relative allowance is at most
\[
\frac{L\Lambda}{T-(L-1)\Lambda}.
\tag{CW45}
\]
In particular a vanishing allowance would follow from L Lambda->0;
that condition has not been proved for actual cycles. Even such an
allowance would not by itself give the required sign. The
[period-surplus audit](juggler_cycle_period_upper_bound.md) records
why a small L Lambda cannot simply be assumed.

### 16. Both terminal boundaries remove the apparent contraction

Use the established actual placement
\[
(m,c_1)\xrightarrow P(h,s)\xrightarrow{OE/EO}(t,q)
\xrightarrow Q(m,c_1).
\]
The gap ranks are 0, o-1, L-1, e-1, 0, with the cut-to-wrap and
wrap-to-seam transitions forming the middle two-step passage. Put
\(g_{\rm cut}=g_{o-1}\), \(g_{\rm seam}=g_{e-1}\).
Equation (CW43) proves
\[
\Delta_P=g_0-g_{\rm cut},\qquad
\Delta_{\rm mixed}=g_{\rm cut}-g_{\rm seam},\qquad
\Delta_Q=g_{\rm seam}-g_0.
\tag{CW46}
\]
Their sum is exactly zero. Equivalently, UV and VU each traverse all
cycle states once and each has total defect Lambda. There is no
uncounted boundary loss to turn this cancellation into a contradiction.

The natural logarithmic loss cone is also uniform but does not repair
this. For a genuine pair under a common word of exponent p, put
\(\lambda_W(x)=p\log x-\log F_W(x)\ge0\),
\(M_W=(\lambda_W(x)+\lambda_W(y))/2\), and
\(D_W=\lambda_W(y)-\lambda_W(x)\). These D_W are differences of
single-log losses, distinct from the log-log arc sums in (CW41).
Then M_W>=0 and |D_W|<=2M_W. Under UV the pair (M,D) changes by
\[
(M,D)_{UV}(I)=p_V(M,D)_U(I)+(M,D)_V(U[I]).
\tag{CW47}
\]
The cone is preserved at every depth, but it is exactly the two
nonnegative endpoint losses and is symmetric in D.

For the mixed operation use the actual OE loss at h and EO loss at s;
both words have exponent 3/4. Thus (CW47) also applies across that
operation without inventing a common word or an actual V(m) return.
The complete pair has exponent \(p=p_P(3/4)p_Q=e^\Lambda\), and
its two endpoint losses are \((p-1)\log m\) and
\((p-1)\log c_1\). Its cone condition reduces to
\(\log(c_1/m)\le\log(mc_1)\), already true for m>1.

One can see the same obstruction directly in secants. Define
\[
\chi_W(x,y)=\frac{\log(F_W(y)/F_W(x))}{p_W\log(y/x)}>0.
\]
It multiplies on the actual image pairs. The mixed factor is exactly
\(\log(q/t)/((3/4)\log(s/h))\), so at closure
\[
\boxed{\chi_P\chi_{\rm mixed}\chi_Q=e^{-\Lambda}.}
\tag{CW48}
\]
Ordinary gap secants differ by the endpoint factor
\(\mathscr L(\mathrm{output})/\mathscr L(\mathrm{input})\), where
\(\mathscr L(a,b)=(b-a)/\log(b/a)\): the ordinary secant is
p_W chi_W times this factor. All these factors cancel around the
complete pair and its ordinary secants multiply to one. Equation
(CW48) therefore expresses exponent surplus and closure, not a new
decrease of the integer gap.

Finally, the raw coefficients in (CW13) retain a signed term absent
from that endpoint cancellation. Write
\(\ell_{\sigma(i)}=A_i\ell_i-f_i\), with the actual lifted
corrections \(f_i=r_i/B_i\). Then
\[
\log\mathcal P=\sum_i\log(1+f_i/\ell_{\sigma(i)}).
\tag{CW49}
\]
Each logarithm is defined because its argument equals the positive
ratio \(A_i\ell_i/\ell_{\sigma(i)}\). This follows by multiplying
the exact gap ratios, including cut and wrap. At an ordinary common
edge, the same ratio is
\(1+(R_{i+1}-R_i)/(c_{\sigma(i+1)}^2-c_{\sigma(i)}^2)\).
Thus (CW48) cannot sign these raw products after the signed remainder
terms have been dropped. A stronger bound needs additional arithmetic
control of those terms on their actual shared states.

## Later scoped refinement

The [quartic shared-remainder continuation](juggler_cycle_quartic_band.md),
Results 34--35, forbids the simultaneous unit remainders in an actual
OE pair modulo7 and tightens the whole-return signed interval.
Results 37--39 go further: a unit incoming O remainder is impossible
already on that edge alone, by a classical Gaussian factorization.
Its positive located charge gives the cut-pair bound Q96 without
double counting and explicitly covers the original cubic terminal
P/mixed/Q arcs. Both endpoints of every terminal interval tighten.

This resolves the allocation omission for split pairs. It does not
orient the signed sum: the same m-landing pair is cut by P and mixed,
included whole by Q, and their coefficients sum to zero on every edge.
The previous independent-box sharpness remains correct for its relaxed
assumptions. The new support kernels are Lean verified; the arithmetic
and actual-cycle interpretation remain audited written proofs.

## Open questions

The joint signed-loss comparison for actual integer cycles remains open.
The uniform bound (CW42) and compulsory-charge refinement (CW44) do not
control residual mass placement on the two exclusive return arcs beyond
the existing budgets. The raw coefficient correction in (CW49) still
depends on those initialized cells. The separate regime \(M\ge m^3\)
is untouched.

## Decision

**CLOSE** the specified real-cell averaging, scalar entropy,
