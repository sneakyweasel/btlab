# Juggler formal vs realized AboveAnchor language

Status: **EXPLORATORY**

Standalone application phase on the Juggler floor-power map. It is
**not** a Research Engine control-layer experiment, not a reopen of
the closed existential word-language census, not a parity-balance
reopen, not a macro-event automaton, not a new atlas language tag,
not Paper A, and not a claim that every positive integer reaches 1.

The shared formal language is already identified: a word is
shared-admissible iff every prefix is noncontracting. This phase
measures the gap between that language and the `AboveAnchor`
prefixes actually generated by integer trajectories.

## Problem

Where does the current shared formal admissibility language first
diverge from the language of integer-realizable `AboveAnchor`
histories, and do the unobserved formal prefixes share a simple
exact law?

## Exact statement

Write \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{formal}}(N)\) for the length-\(N\)
words such that every prefix \(u\) satisfies

\[
2^{|u|}\le 3^{\operatorname{oddCount}(u)}.
\]

This is Lean `prefixNoncontracting`. Isolated-`OE` and initial
`OE` are the same comparison on named shapes. CycleMin-only
exclusions, exact return-to-\(n\), and empirical observations
are excluded.

Write \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{realized}}(N)\) for the length-\(N\)
prefixes \(w\) such that some scanned odd start \(n\) satisfies
`AboveAnchor n w`. Then

\[
\mathcal D_N
=
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{formal}}(N)
\setminus
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{realized}}(N),
\qquad
R_N
=
\frac{|\mathcal L_{\mathrm{realized}}(N)|}
{|\mathcal L_{\mathrm{formal}}(N)|}.
\]

A missing word is

\[
\texttt{NOT\_OBSERVED\_WITHIN\_BOUND},
\]

never global non-realizability. Phase 0 asks whether the minimal
unobserved prefixes \(\mathcal F_j\) admit a simple exact property
\(P\), or whether \(\mathcal D_N\) is as rich as the formal
language (window or floor artefacts).

This is not a halt theorem.

  `AboveAnchor` realizer for odd \(n\le 10^6\).” False:
  \(R_N=1\) for every \(N\le 16\).

  the same lengths.

  parked atlas `REALIZABLE` census.” False: the follows-control
  missing count is \(0\) at every \(N\le 20\).

## Results

Classification **FORMAL_REALIZED_GAP_CLOSED**.

Science window \(k\le 20\), odd \(n\le 10^6\), hold-out split
\(5\cdot 10^5\), leftover laboratories included
(`COMPUTATIONALLY VERIFIED` as a bounded observation):

- \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{formal}}(N)\) fills as an `AboveAnchor`
  language for every \(N\le 16\) (\(R_N=1\)). First unobserved
  prefixes appear at \(N=17\) (\(3\) words).
- At \(N=20\): formal \(27328\), AA \(16822\), dead \(10506\),
  \(R_{20}\approx 0.616\). \(\lvert\mathcal F_j\rvert=8498\),
  concentrated at lengths \(17\)--\(20\).
- No exact predicate on the whole of \(\mathcal F_j\) is
  distinctive against \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{formal}}\).
- Hold-out later fraction \(0.504\): half of the low-\(n\)
  minimal unobserved prefixes realize in the upper half-window.
- Atlas `REALIZABLE` follows-control
  (`wa-20260827T200310Z-cuda-k20-n100000000`): every formal
  word of length \(\le 20\) is observed as a follows-prefix.
  The AA gap is not a follows-language hole.
- Leftover witnesses \(5,37,365\) produce formal `AboveAnchor`
  prefixes. Extra AA words outside \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{formal}}\)
  are \(0\).
- Top-\(S(n)\) starts hit the \(k=20\) cap on diverse words.
  The raw hard-start ratio is a sample-size artefact
  (`HARD_KEEP=32`), not a narrow grammar.

This is the hold-out falsifier and the “no simple \(P\)”
falsifier. The current formal word filter is not losing a
stable forbidden history. The forgotten information, if any,
is not a word-level subset of `prefixNoncontracting`.

## Decision

**CLOSE**. \(\mathcal L_{\mathrm{formal}}\) is the already-named
prefix-noncontracting envelope language. As a follows-language it
fills through length \(20\) on the parked atlas. As an
`AboveAnchor` language it fills through length \(16\) for
\(n\le 10^6\). Later unobserved prefixes have no common exact
\(P\) and half of them realize after a hold-out split. A branch
of that kind is a close.

Best next question: none from this word language. The leftover
hole is still a cube cell without a square cell.
