# Four-coordinate compatibility and the Collatz literature

This document positions the repository's four-coordinate compatibility
milestone against the cited primary sources. Claim labels are **EXACT — HUMAN PROOF**,
**COMPUTATIONALLY VERIFIED**, **CONJECTURE**, and **OBSERVATION**. A theorem
in a cited paper is described as that paper's theorem unless its proof has
also been checked here.

## Common exact notation

For the accelerated odd-only map

\[
T(x)=\frac{3x+1}{2^{v_2(3x+1)}},
\]

let \(\mathbf{k}=(k_0,\ldots,k_{m-1})\), \(K_j=\sum_{i<j}k_i\), and
\(K=K_m\). Repeated substitution gives the **EXACT — HUMAN PROOF** identity

\[
T^m(x)=\frac{3^m x+C(\mathbf{k})}{2^K},\qquad
C(\mathbf{k})=\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}3^{m-1-j}2^{K_j},
\]

equivalently \(C_{\varnothing}=0\) and
\(C_{u\cdot k}=3C_u+2^{K_u}\). Oliver Kramer calls this same affine
constant \(B_m\); throughout this repository,

\[
\boxed{B_m=C(\mathbf{k}).}
\]

## The four exposed coordinates

For one finite code \(\mathbf{k}\), the compatibility state exposes:

1. the refined 2-adic start representative \(R\), the unique integer
   \(1\le R<2^{K+1}\) satisfying
   \[
   R\equiv(2^K-C)3^{-m}\pmod {2^{K+1}};
   \]
2. Kramer's least-positive 3-adic endpoint representative
   \[
   \boxed{M\equiv C\,2^{-K}\pmod {3^m},\qquad1\le M\le3^m;}
   \]
3. the canonical balanced-ternary word \(\operatorname{BT}(R)\);
4. the exact Archimedean drift pair \((3^m,2^K)\).

Kramer's start representative uses the coarser convention

\[
\boxed{r\equiv R\pmod {2^K},\qquad0\le r<2^K.}
\]

The extra bit in \(R\bmod 2^{K+1}\) certifies that the endpoint is odd.
The lift digits are an exact mixed-radix decomposition of \(R\), not a
fifth independent coordinate.

### Exact dependence, not four independent information channels

**EXACT — HUMAN PROOF.** The exponent code determines \(m,K,C\), hence \(R\), \(r\),
\(M\), \(\operatorname{BT}(R)\), the lift digits, and \((3^m,2^K)\).
In particular,

\[
\operatorname{BT}(R)=\operatorname{encode}(R)
\]

is deterministic from \(R\) and the fixed canonical coding rule. It is
therefore not information-theoretically independent of the 2-adic
coordinate. Calling the interface “four-coordinate” means that four exact
views are exposed, not that their joint entropy is the sum of four
entropies.

Lossy exposure may still be useful. A suffix, digit count, run statistic,
hash, or bounded automaton state of \(\operatorname{BT}(R)\) can make a
different finite partition than a chosen lossy residue or rate. That is a
computational feature-engineering fact, not new arithmetic information.

## Kramer: the \(2\)-\(3\)-\(\infty\) diagnostic

Kramer's accelerated notation is

\[
x_m=\frac{3^m x_0+B_m}{2^K},\qquad
B_m=\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}3^{m-1-j}2^{K_j}.
\]

Thus \(B_m=C\). His representatives are exactly

\[
r\equiv-3^{-m}B_m\pmod {2^K}
\]

(equivalent to \(R\bmod2^K\)) and

\[
M\equiv B_m2^{-K}\pmod {3^m}
\]

with \(r\) least nonnegative and \(M\) least positive. His reported rates
use the natural logarithm:

\[
d_m=\left|\frac Km-\frac{\ln3}{\ln2}\right|,\qquad
\rho_r(m)=\frac{\ln(1+r)}m,
\]

\[
\rho_M(m)=\frac1m\ln\!\left(
1+\frac{M}{(3/2)^m}\right).
\]

**THEOREM IN KRAMER.** If an infinite code is generated by one fixed
positive odd integer, then eventually \(r=x_0\), eventually \(M=x_m\), and
\(\rho_r(m),\rho_M(m)\to0\). The proof uses
\(x_m+1\le(x_0+1)(3/2)^m\).

Kramer's searches at finite lengths are **COMPUTATIONALLY VERIFIED** in
that work. Persistent positive rates in those experiments are not a proof
of incompatibility for every infinite code. This repository implements the
exact representatives and labels the floating rates as diagnostics.

Primary source: Oliver Kramer,
[Adaptive Search in Collatz Exponent-Code Space via 2-adic and 3-adic Constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10041),
preprint.

## Rational base \(3/2\)

For the rational-base representation of \(n\), set \(n_0=n\) and recursively
choose \(a_i\in\{0,1,2\}\) and \(n_{i+1}\ge0\) by

\[
2n_i=3n_{i+1}+a_i.
\]

Then

\[
n=\sum_{i\ge0}\frac{a_i}{2}\left(\frac32\right)^i
\]

with only finitely many nonzero terms. Eliahou and Verger-Gaugry prove
that for a valid word \(w=\langle n\rangle_{3/2}\),

\[
w1\text{ is valid}\iff n\equiv1\pmod2,
\qquad
w1=\left\langle\frac{3n+1}{2}\right\rangle_{3/2}.
\]

Thus an odd step of the usual map, \(n\mapsto(3n+1)/2\), appends the digit
\(1\) exactly. The accelerated map additionally removes all remaining
powers of two; the append theorem alone does not encode that normalization.

Primary source: Shalom Eliahou and Jean-Louis Verger-Gaugry,
[The number system in rational base \(3/2\) and the \(3x+1\) problem](https://doi.org/10.5802/crmath.662),
*Comptes Rendus Mathématique* 363 (2025), 329–336.

## Dhiman–Pandey: exact scope of the automata obstruction

For odd prime \(q\) and odd \(d\), Dhiman and Pandey study

\[
T_{q,d}(n)=
\begin{cases}
n/2,&n\text{ even},\\
(qn+d)/2,&n\text{ odd}.
\end{cases}
\]

Their theorem concerns only the **arbitrary-step** ternary relation

\[
\mathcal T=\{(x,z,P):P=2^k,\ z=T_{q,d}^{\,k}(x),\ k\in\mathbb N\}.
\]

**THEOREM IN DHIMAN–PANDEY.** \(\mathcal T\) is not first-order definable
in base-2 Büchi arithmetic \(BA_2\), hence no finite automaton reading
binary representations recognizes that entire arbitrary-step relation.
The reduction extracts \(\{q^y:y\in\mathbb N\}\) and invokes the
Cobham–Semënov theorem.

This does **not** say that a one-step map, a fixed-\(k\) relation, one
valuation cylinder, a bounded precision-drop recognizer, or the
repository's fixed-valuation balanced-ternary transducers are
undefinable. It must not be cited for any of those stronger statements.

Primary source: Madhav Dhiman and Rohan Pandey,
[Logical Undefinability of the Generalized Collatz Transition Relation in Büchi Arithmetic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12772),
preprint, v2.

## Rozier–Terracol: theorem, computation, conjecture

Rozier and Terracol use the parity map

\[
\mathcal T(n)=
\begin{cases}
n/2,&n\text{ even},\\
(3n+1)/2,&n\text{ odd},
\end{cases}
\]

and call a length-\(j\) segment paradoxical when, with \(q\) odd steps,

\[
\frac{3^q}{2^j}<1
\quad\text{but}\quad
\mathcal T^j(n)\ge n.
\]

The distinctions are essential:

- **Proved in the paper:** if only finitely many paradoxical sequences
  exist, then the Collatz conjecture follows.
- **COMPUTATIONAL PART OF THE PAPER:** exactly 593 paradoxical sequences
  start at \(n\le4614\), and none further occur in the checked range
  \(3\le n\le2.8\times10^{19}\).
- **CONJECTURE IN THE PAPER:** no paradoxical sequence starts above 4614.

The finite census does not prove the conjecture. Its coefficient/remainder
split is closely related to this repository's exact split into the
homogeneous drift \(3^m/2^K\) and affine term \(C/2^K\), but neither split
is a Lyapunov function.

Primary source: Olivier Rozier and Claude Terracol,
[Paradoxical behavior in Collatz sequences](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2026.115167),
*Discrete Mathematics* (2026); see also
[arXiv:2502.00948](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.00948).

## Balanced-ternary word permutations

Milestone 9 treats OEIS reversal \(W\) as a permutation of canonical
balanced-ternary words, not as a fifth numerical coordinate of an exponent
code. This is a different object from:

- the 2-adic cylinder of a valuation word;
- Kramer's 3-adic endpoint representative \(M\);
- canonical rational base \(3/2\), whose odd step appends a trailing `1`
  rather than reversing a balanced-ternary word.

No exact identity identifying \(W(n)\) or \(W(R(\mathbf{k}))\) with those
coordinates was found. The comparison is therefore a dictionary of
different word operations, not a novelty claim. See
[collatz_bt_warp.md](collatz_bt_warp.md).

## Fixed-integer affine-center geometry

Milestone 10 does not add a new 2-adic start representative, a new 3-adic
endpoint, or a new critical-slope calculation. Kramer already uses the
affine constant, the pair \((3^m,2^K)\), and the representatives \(r\) and
\(M\). The intended narrower object is the coupled exact state of **one
fixed integer**

\[
(C_m,\,K_m,\,n_m,\,n_{*m},\,G_m),\qquad
G_m=2^{K_m}(n-n_m).
\]

That rewriting shows \(n_{*m}\le n\) is the stopping-time comparison in
the contracting regime, not a positivity law, and it fails at \(n=165\).
The normalized series \(A_m=C_m/3^m\) is Kramer's affine constant in
homogeneous units. Required start residues are the existing cylinder.
See [collatz_fixed_integer_asymptotics.md](collatz_fixed_integer_asymptotics.md).
